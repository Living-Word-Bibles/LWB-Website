The license does not transfer ownership of LWB software, artwork, trademarks, editorial content, metadata, or any Scripture text. It does not authorize source-code redistribution, cloning, bulk export, scraping, automated harvesting, offline repackaging, white-label resale, or use on unapproved domains.
