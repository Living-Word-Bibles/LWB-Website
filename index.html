The classic English Catholic translation from the Latin Vulgate—beloved for prayer, study, and devotional reading. This edition includes the full Catholic canon of 73 books, featuring Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach (Ecclesiasticus), Baruch, 1 Maccabees, and 2 Maccabees, together with the traditional Greek additions to Esther and Daniel.

Why This Bible Matters

The New Testament was published at Reims in 1582 and the Old Testament at Douay in 1609–1610. Later Challoner revisions shaped the form read by generations of Catholics.
