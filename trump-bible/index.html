Foreword

One of only 2 Bibles Officially Endorsed by President Donald J. Trump

America is a nation with a remarkable history, an extraordinary inheritance, and a future that depends upon each generation understanding what came before it.

At the heart of that inheritance are two great traditions that have shaped the lives of millions of Americans: faith in God and devotion to liberty.

The Holy Bible has brought comfort to generations in times of hardship, wisdom in times of uncertainty, strength in times of trial, and hope when the path ahead seemed difficult. Its words have been read in churches, homes, schools, hospitals, battlefields, prisons, and countless places where men & women have turned to Almighty God for guidance.

For centuries, the King James Version has held a special place in the English-speaking world. Its language has influenced our literature, our culture, our public life, and the way generations of believers have encountered the Word of God.

This edition preserves that great biblical tradition while bringing together several of the documents that tell the story of the American Republic.

The Declaration of Independence proclaimed to the world that human beings possess rights that do not come from kings or governments. Its famous words recognize that all men are created equal and endowed by their Creator with certain unalienable rights, including Life, Liberty, and the pursuit of Happiness.

Those principles gave birth to the United States of America.

The Constitution that followed created the framework of our government and established a system designed to separate the powers of government, preserve representative democracy, and protect the liberty of the American people. The amendments that became part of that Constitution—including the Bill of Rights—remain central to American civic life.

One of the greatest freedoms protected by our Constitution is the freedom to worship Almighty God.

Religious liberty is not a small part of the American story. It is at the very heart of who we are as a people. Americans must always have the right to pray, to worship, to read the Bible, to speak openly about their faith, and to live according to their deeply held religious beliefs without fear that the government will infringe upon their rights for doing so.

That freedom is precious.

It is also something we must protect.

America has always been a nation of faith. We are a nation of prayer. We are a nation under God. From the earliest days of our country, Americans have turned to God in moments of celebration, in moments of tragedy, in times of war, and in times of peace. Our people have prayed before great battles, before great decisions, around family tables, inside churches, and in the privacy of their own homes.

And throughout our history, the Bible has been there.

The Bible has helped shape countless generations of American families. It has given strength to our soldiers. It has comforted those who were suffering. It has inspired pastors, presidents, parents, teachers, workers, and millions of ordinary Americans who wanted to live good and faithful lives.

We live in a time when the world is moving very quickly. Technology is changing. Governments change. Leaders come and go. Nations rise and nations fall. But the Word of God has endured.

That is something very powerful.

The Bible teaches us about faith, courage, sacrifice, forgiveness, justice, mercy, family, leadership, and the difference between right and wrong. It tells us about great victories and terrible failures. It tells us about people who remained faithful when the world was against them and people who discovered that enormous success means very little if you lose your way.

Most importantly, the Bible directs our hearts and our minds toward Almighty God.

Our Founding Fathers created an extraordinary system of government, but they understood something fundamental: government does not give us our most basic rights. Our rights come from our Creator. Government has the responsibility to respect and protect those rights.

We can never forget the soldiers who defended our country on battlefields around the world. We can never forget the families who waited for them at home. We can never forget the Americans who built our cities, worked our farms, started businesses, raised families, founded churches, created charities, taught our children, and made this country stronger with every generation.

America became a great nation because generations of Americans believed that liberty was worth defending, family was worth protecting, faith was worth practicing, and the future was worth building.

Those values still endure today.

When America remembers who we are, when our families are strong, when our people are free, when our churches are open, when our citizens understand their rights, and when we are not ashamed to put our trust in Almighty God, there is no limit to what our country can accomplish.

That is the American spirit.

I hope you will always remember that the freedoms we enjoy are precious, that they must be defended, and that our greatest strength has always come from the faith, courage, and determination of the American people.

May this Bible bring strength to your family, deepen your faith, and remind you of the extraordinary country we have been blessed to call home. May we always remain One Nation Under God. May God bless you, may God bless your family, and may Almighty God continue to bless the United States of America.

Donald J. Trump
45th & 47th President of the United States
