1. Equal Opportunity Employer Notice

EQUAL OPPORTUNITY EMPLOYER

Living Word Bibles does not unlawfully discriminate based on race, color, religion, sex, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, military or veteran status, citizenship or immigration status where protected, or any other characteristic protected by applicable federal, state, or local law.

Employment practices are administered consistently with applicable law, including Title VII, the ADA, the ADEA, GINA, the Equal Pay Act, USERRA, and, where applicable, VEVRAA and the Rehabilitation Act.

2. Equal Employment Opportunity Policy

Policy Statement

Living Word Bibles is committed to a workplace free from unlawful discrimination, harassment, and retaliation and to equal employment opportunity for qualified applicants and employees. This policy applies to recruitment, hiring, assignments, promotion, transfer, compensation, benefits, training, discipline, termination, and other terms and conditions of employment.

Anti-Harassment

Unlawful harassment, including sexual harassment, is prohibited whether committed by supervisors, coworkers, contractors, vendors, customers, visitors, or others interacting with LWB personnel in a work-related setting. Prohibited conduct may be verbal, physical, visual, written, digital, or online.

Reasonable Accommodation

LWB provides reasonable accommodations to qualified applicants and employees with disabilities as required by law and considers accommodations for sincerely held religious beliefs, observances, and practices as required by law.

Reporting & Non-Retaliation

Anyone who believes they experienced or witnessed discrimination, harassment, or retaliation should report the matter promptly to Human Resources or management. Good-faith reporting, accommodation requests, and participation in reviews are protected from unlawful retaliation.

3. Veteran-Owned Business & Veteran Employment Policy

Living Word Bibles
Operated by Cook Services Company, LLC
Effective Date: 30 August 2026
Applies To: Applicants and employees of Living Word Bibles and its controlled operations, as applicable.

1. Policy Statement

Living Word Bibles is operated by Cook Services Company, LLC, a veteran-owned business. LWB recognizes the value, judgment, leadership, public-service commitment, technical experience, and discipline veterans bring to the workplace.

LWB provides equal employment opportunity to qualified persons, including veterans and service members, and makes employment decisions without unlawful discrimination based on military or veteran status. LWB supports recruiting, hiring, training, developing, and promoting qualified veterans and service members consistent with applicable law and business needs.

2. Covered Veteran Categories

For this policy, a veteran means a person who served on active duty in the United States ground, air, naval, or space services and was discharged or released under conditions other than dishonorable.

This policy recognizes protected-veteran categories used in federal law and related regulations, including:

Disabled VeteranA veteran who served on active duty in the U.S. ground, air, naval, or space services and is entitled to compensation under laws administered by the Department of Veterans Affairs, or who was released from active duty because of a service-connected disability.
Recently Separated VeteranA veteran who served on active duty in the U.S. ground, air, naval, or space services and was discharged or released during the three-year period beginning on the date of discharge or release from active duty.
Active-Duty Wartime or Campaign Badge VeteranA veteran who served on active duty in the U.S. ground, air, naval, or space services during a war or in a campaign or expedition for which a campaign badge, service medal, or expeditionary medal was authorized.
Armed Forces Service Medal VeteranA veteran who served on active duty in the U.S. ground, air, naval, or space services and participated in a qualifying U.S. military operation for which an Armed Forces Service Medal was awarded under applicable authority, including Executive Order 12985.

3. Equal Employment Opportunity for Veterans

LWB prohibits unlawful discrimination against applicants or employees because of military or veteran status. Employment decisions are based on merit, qualifications, experience, business needs, and applicable law. This commitment applies to recruitment, hiring, onboarding, assignments, compensation and benefits, training and development, promotion opportunities, discipline, termination, and other terms and conditions of employment.

No applicant or employee will be subjected to unlawful harassment, intimidation, retaliation, or adverse treatment because of protected military or veteran status.

4. Veteran Outreach

LWB may support veteran employment and professional opportunity through veteran-focused recruiting and outreach; recognition of transferable military leadership, communications, logistics, intelligence, technical, security, administrative, and operational experience; support for qualified veterans moving into technology, publishing, business, administration, investment support, e-commerce, and related operations; and reasonable accommodation for veterans with disabilities where required by law.

5. Reemployment and Military Service

LWB respects rights and obligations associated with military service and administers military leave, reemployment, and related employment matters consistently with USERRA and other applicable law.

6. Confidentiality

Information voluntarily provided by an applicant or employee regarding veteran status will be handled as confidentially as reasonably practicable and used for legitimate employment, accommodation, reporting, or compliance purposes.

7. Questions and Reporting

Questions about this policy or concerns about possible violations may be directed to Human Resources. Applicants and employees may also consult the U.S. Department of Labor, the U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, or, where applicable, the Office of Federal Contract Compliance Programs for information about military and veteran employment rights.

4. Workplace Safety / OSHA Policy

LWB is committed to a safe, healthy, and professional work environment and to compliance with applicable workplace safety and health requirements. Personnel are expected to work safely, report unsafe conditions promptly, use equipment responsibly, participate in required training, and cooperate with safety reviews.

LWB prohibits unlawful retaliation against employees for raising good-faith safety concerns or exercising rights protected by applicable safety law.

5. Alcohol & Drug-Free Workplace Policy

LWB is committed to a safe, healthy, professional, and productive workplace. The unlawful use, possession, sale, distribution, or manufacture of controlled substances during work time, on company property, or while performing company business is prohibited. Employees may not perform work while impaired in a manner that creates a safety, performance, or professional-conduct concern.

Lawfully prescribed and over-the-counter medication should be used as directed. Where permitted by law, appropriate fitness-for-duty or testing procedures may be used in circumstances supported by legitimate safety or business considerations.

6. Reporting Concerns

Applicants and employees may report concerns involving discrimination, harassment, retaliation, accommodation, military or veteran status, unsafe working conditions, workplace impairment, or other potential policy violations. LWB prohibits retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report, participates in a review, requests an accommodation, raises a safety concern, or exercises rights protected by law.
