2. Public-Domain Scripture

LWB does not claim ownership merely because a public-domain Bible is displayed in an LWB reader or packaged in an LWB edition. Anyone may independently obtain and lawfully reuse public-domain Scripture from a lawful source. LWB rights in its own edition do not convert the underlying text into proprietary Scripture.

Separate layers of rights. A public-domain Bible text may be free to reuse while an LWB cover, interface, annotation, metadata set, product description, software implementation, or original edition design remains protected.
