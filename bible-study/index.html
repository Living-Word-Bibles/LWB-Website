Common Christian Prayers

Prayer joins Scripture study with worship, confession, thanksgiving, and dependence on God. We are building a dedicated prayer library from the prayers already featured on Christian Living.

The Lord’s Prayer

The prayer Jesus taught His disciples and one of Christianity’s foundational patterns of prayer.

Hail Mary

A historic Christian prayer drawing on the angelic greeting and Elizabeth’s blessing in Luke.

The Serenity Prayer

A prayer for acceptance, courage, wisdom, surrender, and trust in God.

The Nicene Creed

A historic confession of Christian faith used prayerfully in worship across many traditions.

The Glory Be

A brief doxology of praise to the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

The Guardian Angel Prayer

A traditional prayer asking for God’s guarding care through His angels.

Our Approach

Scripture First

Living Word Bibles approaches Bible study with reverence for Scripture and respect for its historical, literary, linguistic, and theological context. Biblical text is kept distinct from editorial commentary. Study material should identify interpretation as interpretation, acknowledge meaningful differences among Christian traditions when relevant, and use responsible scholarship without allowing commentary to replace the Word itself.
