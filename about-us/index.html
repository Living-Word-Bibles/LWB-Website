Our Story

Living Word Bibles is an independent Bible publisher rooted in Indiana. We create simple, elegant, reverent editions of the Holy Bible designed to be read often—at home, in church, and on the go.

Since 2000, our work has centered on access: placing God’s Word within reach across websites, phones, tablets, and e-readers.

Beautifully Formatted to Bring God’s Word to Life on Any Device

Every edition is prepared for readability, clear navigation, and faithful engagement with Scripture. Living Word Bibles is non-denominational at heart and seeks to encourage Christians from many traditions to walk closely with Christ.

Frequently Asked Questions

Living Word Bibles is an independent Bible publisher focused on accessible, reverent, and readable digital editions of Scripture.

The online directory includes fifteen translations, including KJV, NKJV, NIV, ESV, NET, LEB, ASV, YLT, WEB, OEB, DBY, GNV, BSB, BBE, DRB.

The online Bible readers are available without a reading fee. Some downloadable or specially formatted editions are free, while others are sold at low cost.

Visit the Living Word Bibles App page for installation and purchase instructions for iPhone, iPad, Android, and desktop.

Underlying public-domain Scripture may be reused according to law, but Living Word Bibles formatting, design, editorial material, artwork, metadata, and navigation remain protected. Review the Copyright Notice and Licensing pages.

Living Word Bibles does not sell or distribute KJV editions into the United Kingdom because of the jurisdiction-specific rights associated with the Authorized Version.
