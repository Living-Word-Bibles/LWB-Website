About Living Word Bibles

Beautifully Formatted to Bring God’s Word to Life on Any Device

Living Word Bibles is an independent Bible publisher rooted in Indiana. We create simple, elegant, reverent editions of the Holy Bible designed to be read often—at home, in church, and on the go.

Since 2000, our work has centered on access: placing God’s Word within reach across websites, phones, tablets, e-readers, audio, and carefully selected print editions.

Our Mission

Designed to Make Scripture Clear, Accessible, and Enduring

Every edition is prepared for readability, clear navigation, and faithful engagement with Scripture. Living Word Bibles is non-denominational at heart and seeks to encourage Christians from many traditions to walk closely with Christ.

Accessible by Design

Scripture should be easy to reach, read, navigate, and return to across modern devices.

Reverent Presentation

Design should serve the biblical text—never overwhelm it—and support thoughtful reading and devotion.

Scripture First

Our publishing, study, history, and devotional resources exist to bring readers back to the Word itself.

More Ways to Experience the Bible

Living Word Bibles now brings together online reading, a dedicated KJV listening experience, digital editions, and selected print Bibles in one growing Scripture platform.

KJV Audio Bible

Listen to the King James Version

The Living Word Bibles KJV Audio Bible pairs public-domain LibriVox narration with simple book, chapter, and verse navigation. As the audio plays, the listening interface rotates through a growing library of historic public-domain Bible artwork, creating a Scripture experience designed for listening at home, during travel, or anywhere reading is not practical.
