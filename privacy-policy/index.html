1. Scope & Controller

This Policy applies to livingwordbibles.com, Bible readers, eStore, account pages, product downloads, audio Bible, Ethiopian Bible products, support, newsletters, licensing services, and other LWB services that link to this Policy.

The responsible operating entity is generally Cook Services Company, LLC, doing business as Living Word Bibles, 151 N. Delaware Street, Suite 122, Indianapolis, Indiana 46204, unless another entity is expressly identified for a particular activity.

2. Information We Collect

Identifiers & Contact Data

Name, email address, account/customer identifiers, contact details, support information, and newsletter status.

Account Data

Verification status, authentication records, account status, session metadata, password hashes for customer accounts, library entitlements, and account activity.

Commercial & Transaction Data

Products, order identifiers, PayPal transaction/capture identifiers, price, currency, payment status, refunds, account reconciliation, download and entitlement records.

Technical & Activity Data

Page path, page views, clicks, form-submit events, safe link destinations, element labels, browser or device information, timestamps, and security or diagnostic metadata.

3. Site Activity & System Logging

LWB uses its existing System Log and related technical records to understand site operation, diagnose errors, audit important activity, deter misuse, support security, and improve service reliability. Depending on context, logs may record a page view, click, form-submit event, page path, safe destination path, element type or label, timestamp, browser/device information, authenticated customer identity, or authenticated administrator identity.

Current activity logger exclusions. Ordinary click/page telemetry is designed not to record typed form values, passwords, password-reset or email-verification tokens, or URL query strings. LWB may separately process information a user intentionally submits through an account, contact, newsletter, purchase, reconciliation, or support form because that information is necessary to perform the requested function.

4. Accounts & Authentication

LWB processes account information to register users, verify email addresses, authenticate sessions, provide libraries and downloads, reset passwords, prevent fraud, and provide support. Passwords for ordinary customer accounts are processed as security credentials and are not displayed back to users.

Local or session browser storage may hold a signed account session token and limited cached account-display information so the site can maintain sign-in state.

5. Payments & Purchase Reconciliation

PayPal may process paid transactions. LWB may receive payer email, transaction identifiers, product information, payment status, amount, currency, residence or country information, and related transaction data needed for fulfillment, accounting, fraud prevention, customer support, and account entitlements.

When a user submits a PayPal transaction ID to reconcile a purchase, LWB may compare that identifier against PayPal verification data and LWB Orders, Order Items, Products, Customers, and Entitlements records. If verified and eligible, the purchase may be associated with the user's account. Print/physical purchases do not automatically create digital entitlements.

6. Newsletter & Communications

LWB processes subscriber email addresses, names where provided, subscription status, source, consent information, unsubscribe history, and campaign records to send and manage newsletters. Unsubscribed addresses may be retained on a Do Not Email or suppression record so the opt-out can be honored.

Opting out of newsletters does not necessarily stop necessary account, security, order, verification, password-reset, reconciliation, legal, or support messages.

7. Administrative Portal & Audit Records

Authorized administrators may use LWB's private portal to manage subscribers, compose newsletters, review customer accounts, reconcile purchases, and grant or revoke eligible account entitlements. Administrative actions may be recorded in System Log or related existing business records with the administrator identity, event type, affected customer or record, timestamp, source, and limited metadata needed for audit and security.

Portal access is restricted to authorized administrative personnel. Internal administrative settings and credentials are treated as operational information and are not intended for public display.

8. Cookies, Local Storage & Similar Technologies

LWB may use browser local storage, session storage, cookies, or comparable mechanisms for account sessions, interface state, preferences, fraud prevention, security, analytics or advertising where enabled, and other functionality. Third parties may use their own technologies under their policies.

9. How We Use Information

  • Provide Bible readers, accounts, downloads, eBibles, PDFs, audio features, and support.
  • Verify accounts, transactions, product eligibility, reconciliation requests, and entitlements.
  • Send newsletters and transactional communications.
  • Protect users, LWB, publishers, and the integrity of the Services.
  • Audit administrative actions, troubleshoot errors, prevent fraud, and investigate abuse.
  • Comply with legal, accounting, publisher, licensing, tax, or regulatory obligations.

10. How We Disclose Information

LWB may disclose information to service providers, payment processors, hosting or technical providers, professional advisers, publishers/rightsholders where necessary to address a licensing matter, law-enforcement or government authorities where legally required, and business successors subject to applicable law. LWB does not provide advertisers with private customer conversations or sell subscriber lists as part of its ordinary business model.

11. Retention

Retention varies by record and purpose. Account, order, entitlement, newsletter-suppression, audit, security, publisher, accounting, and legal records may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable business, ministry, contractual, legal, fraud-prevention, or compliance purpose. Some records may be retained after account closure or unsubscribe when needed to preserve transaction history or honor an opt-out.

12. Security

LWB uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the Services, but no Internet or storage system is perfectly secure. Users should protect credentials and notify LWB of suspected unauthorized account activity.

13. Children

LWB's general public Bible content may be read by families and young people, but account creation, paid transactions, and other contractual activities may require an adult or appropriate legal authorization. LWB does not knowingly use the Services to solicit unnecessary sensitive information from children.

14. Privacy Rights & Choices

Depending on jurisdiction and applicable thresholds, individuals may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, or information about processing. Newsletter recipients may opt out through the unsubscribe link or Opt-Out page.

15. International Users

LWB is operated from the United States. International users may have additional mandatory rights under applicable data-protection law. Where those rights apply, this Policy is intended to be interpreted consistently with them.

16. Changes to This Policy

LWB may revise this Policy to reflect changes in accounts, payment reconciliation, logging, administrative tools, newsletters, technology, law, or business practices. The revision date above identifies the current published version.
