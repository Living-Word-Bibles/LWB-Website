1. Purpose & Scope

Living Word Bibles presents Bible translations for reading, study, discovery, digital products, and other lawful uses. This Notice is directed principally to Bible publishers, translation owners, licensors, trademark owners, and authorized agents.

Where a written license, permission letter, API agreement, open license, public-domain status, statute, or other controlling instrument applies, that controlling authority prevails over an inconsistent public summary.

2. Relationship to App Licensing

The public App Licensing program is intentionally narrower than LWB's publisher-facing use of translations. The standard public web embed license is KJV only.

Non-KJV implementations may be considered only for churches and religious nonprofit organizations and only when consistent with the applicable copyright holder's license terms and the scope of LWB's own permission. Such availability does not create a general sublicense or permit a church or nonprofit to exceed the rightsholder's rules.

4. Church / Religious Nonprofit Implementations

LWB may provide a non-KJV reader or embed to a church or religious nonprofit only after reviewing the requested translation, intended domain or app, jurisdiction, audience, monetization, attribution, and other license conditions. LWB may require written confirmation of nonprofit or church status and may impose conditions narrower than the publisher's maximum allowance.

No ownership transfer and no implied sublicense. A church/nonprofit implementation remains subject to the rightsholder's current copyright, trademark, quotation, territory, distribution, attribution, and technical terms.

5. Text Integrity & Provenance

LWB seeks to preserve the authorized text edition, required copyright lines, translation identifiers, verse numbering, book/chapter mapping, and other integrity requirements applicable to each translation. Presentation changes are intended to concern responsive display, accessibility, navigation, and interface behavior rather than unauthorized textual rewriting.

When a publisher changes a text edition, attribution, API, domain rule, or delivery method, LWB may update or restrict the implementation accordingly.

6. Territorial Restrictions

Rights can vary by country. LWB may restrict a translation, download, product, embed, advertisement, or other feature in a territory where a rightsholder, law, Crown right, contract, or platform rule requires different treatment.

7. Technical Controls

LWB may use rate limits, domain allowlists, tokenized downloads, authentication, entitlement checks, anti-scraping measures, API restrictions, logging, or other proportionate controls to comply with licenses and protect service integrity.

8. Publisher Changes & Takedowns

A rightsholder may request correction of an attribution, text edition, territory rule, product, cover, trademark reference, API implementation, or other use. LWB may temporarily restrict affected content while reviewing a credible request. Requests should identify the right claimed, the affected URL or product, the requested correction, and contact information for the rights representative.

9. Controlling License / No Waiver

This Notice is informational. It does not waive rights, create estoppel, amend a contract, or expand LWB's permission. A controlling written license or rightsholder instruction prevails to the extent of any conflict.
