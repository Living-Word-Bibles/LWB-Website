Psalms 19:14 (KJV)

In the wisdom literature, Psalms 19:14 gives counsel that is both poetic and intensely practical. The psalmist and sage train hearts that fear the LORD how to pray, praise, and live skillfully amid trouble and temptation.

Use Psalms 19:14 as a pattern for prayer and as a compass for daily choices. Wisdom texts pair promise with responsibility—calling us to trust the LORD, walk in His paths, and anchor hope in His character.
