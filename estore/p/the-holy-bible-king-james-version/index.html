First published in 1611, the King James Version (KJV) of the Holy Bible is one of the most influential and beloved translations of Scripture in the English-speaking world. Known for its majestic language, poetic cadence, and faithful rendering of the original texts, the KJV has inspired readers for over four centuries and remains a cornerstone of Christian devotion and study.

This edition presents the complete text of the Holy Bible: King James Version, thoughtfully formatted for a smooth and accessible digital reading experience.
