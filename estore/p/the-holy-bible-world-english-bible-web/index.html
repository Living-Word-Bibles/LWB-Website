The World English Bible (WEB) presents the timeless Scriptures in clear, contemporary English. Faithful to the original Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek, the WEB retains dignity while removing dated language. It is ideal for personal reading, study, teaching, and outreach.

The WEB is a modern-English translation designed for clarity, accuracy, and broad usability. Carefully revised from historic public-domain sources over many years, it renders God’s Word in natural, readable English without sacrificing fidelity to the biblical languages. Archaisms are removed, grammar and punctuation are modernized, and idioms are expressed in contemporary language so that both new readers and lifelong students can engage the text with fresh understanding.

Because the underlying WEB text is public domain, it can be quoted, shared, printed, and distributed without ordinary translation-licensing restrictions, making it a practical choice for churches, classrooms, and outreach.
