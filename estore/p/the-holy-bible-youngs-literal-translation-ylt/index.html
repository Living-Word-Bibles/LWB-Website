Experience the timeless Word of God in one of the most faithful and literal translations ever produced.

The Holy Bible: Young’s Literal Translation (YLT) is the life’s work of Dr. Robert Young, a renowned biblical scholar and author of Young’s Analytical Concordance. First published in the late nineteenth century, the YLT remains a treasured resource for readers who desire a translation that adheres as closely as possible to the original Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek texts.

Unlike many modern versions, the YLT avoids paraphrase and interpretation, offering instead a word-for-word rendering that reveals the structure, rhythm, and meaning of Scripture with remarkable clarity. This makes it especially valuable for serious Bible study, comparative reading, and anyone seeking to examine the Scriptures in a highly literal form.
