Mark 10:27 (KJV)

Mark 10:27 sits within the ministry of Jesus, revealing His identity and mission. Read in the KJV, the cadence and diction invite meditation on the Lord’s words and works. In the Gospel setting, this verse is not an isolated slogan; it connects to what comes before and after.

Practically, Mark 10:27 calls the reader to respond to Christ with trust and allegiance. The verse becomes a touchstone for evangelism and discipleship—holding together God’s love, faith in the Son, and the promise held out to those who believe.
