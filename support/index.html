Orders & Payments

Help with Living Word Bibles purchases, PayPal checkout, billing descriptors, downloads, and duplicate charges.

Having Issues With Your Order?

If you are having trouble with a Living Word Bibles order, digital download, Bible App purchase, payment, or access to a purchased product, contact Support.

  • Include the email address used for your purchase.
  • Tell us which product you purchased.
  • Include the approximate purchase date.
  • Include your PayPal transaction ID if available.

How Payments Are Processed

Payments for products purchased directly from Living Word Bibles are processed securely through PayPal. Living Word Bibles does not require customers to send payment-card information by email.

Why does my statement say “Cook Services Co” or “CSC”?

Living Word Bibles is operated by Cook Services Company. A Living Word Bibles purchase may therefore appear on your PayPal account or financial statement under the billing descriptor Cook Services Co or CSC.

I paid but cannot access my purchase

First confirm that PayPal shows the payment as completed. If your payment succeeded but your download or product access did not appear, contact Support with your transaction ID and purchase email.

I think I was charged more than once

Please do not place another order. Contact Support with the purchase email and all relevant PayPal transaction IDs so the transactions can be reviewed.

Orders Through Other Retailers

Orders placed through Amazon or another third-party retailer are processed and fulfilled by that retailer. Questions about payment, shipping, refunds, order status, or retailer-specific purchases should generally be directed to the retailer where the order was placed.

Purchase & Download Troubleshooting

Try these steps before contacting Technical Support.

Confirm the PayPal payment

Check PayPal to make sure the payment shows as completed rather than pending, cancelled, or declined.

Return to Living Word Bibles

If PayPal provides a return or continuation option after checkout, follow it so the purchase flow can finish normally.

Refresh the page

Reload the Living Word Bibles page once. If you are using a login, confirm that you are signed in to the expected account.

Try another browser or device

If a download or page will not open, try a current version of Safari, Chrome, Edge, or another supported browser.

Copy the page URL

Send Support the exact page address where the issue occurred.

Include a screenshot if useful

A screenshot can help us identify a payment, download, layout, installation, or browser problem more quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Living Word Bibles support questions.

Having Issues With Your Order?

If you are having trouble with a Living Word Bibles order, digital download, Bible App purchase, payment, or access to a purchased product, please contact Living Word Bibles Support or Cook Services Company Support.

Please include the email used for your purchase, the product, approximate purchase date, and PayPal transaction ID if available.

How are payments processed?

Payments for products purchased directly from Living Word Bibles are processed securely through PayPal. Living Word Bibles does not require customers to send payment information by email.

Why does my bank, card, or PayPal statement say “Cook Services Co” or “CSC”?

Living Word Bibles is operated by Cook Services Company. Purchases made through Living Word Bibles may therefore appear on your PayPal account or financial statement under the billing descriptor Cook Services Co or CSC.

I paid for a product but cannot access or download it. What should I do?

Confirm that PayPal shows the payment as completed. If you received confirmation but cannot access the Living Word Bibles purchase, contact Support with your PayPal transaction ID and purchase email.

I think I was charged more than once. What should I do?

Please do not place another order. Contact Living Word Bibles Support with the purchase email and any PayPal transaction IDs associated with the charges.

I purchased a product through another retailer. Can Living Word Bibles help with that order?

Orders placed through Amazon or another third-party retailer are processed and fulfilled by that retailer. Questions involving payment, shipping, refunds, order status, or retailer-specific purchases should generally be directed to the company through which the order was placed.

Is thelivingword.shop associated with Living Word Bibles?

No. Living Word Bibles is not affiliated with, operated by, sponsored by, or associated with thelivingword.shop.

If you purchased an Ethiopian Bible or another product from that website, please contact thelivingword.shop support regarding your order.

I ordered an Ethiopian Bible from thelivingword.shop. Can Living Word Bibles locate my order?

No. Living Word Bibles cannot view, modify, cancel, refund, fulfill, or provide order status for transactions made through that website. Please contact thelivingword.shop support.

How can I tell whether I am using the official Living Word Bibles website?

Use the official Living Word Bibles website. Before entering payment information, verify the website address carefully.

Does Living Word Bibles offer the Ethiopian Bible?

Yes. Visit the official Living Word Bibles Ethiopian Bible page for Living Word Bibles resources and products.

Where can I get the Living Word Bibles App?

Visit the Living Word Bibles App page for current installation and purchase information.

Is the Living Word Bibles App available on Android?

Visit the Living Word Bibles App page for current supported Android installation instructions.

I found an error or problem in an online Bible. How do I report it?

Please email Living Word Bibles Support and include, when applicable:

  • Bible translation
  • Book
  • Chapter and verse
  • Page URL
  • Device or browser
  • Description of the problem
Can my church use Living Word Bibles on its website?

Visit the For Churches and Licensing pages for available options and permitted uses.

Can I use Living Word Bibles content in my own website or app?

The rights applicable to individual Bible translations vary, while Living Word Bibles formatting, design, artwork, editorial material, navigation, metadata, software, and other original content may be separately protected. Review the Copyright Notice and Licensing pages before reuse.

Why can’t I purchase the KJV in the United Kingdom?

Living Word Bibles does not sell or distribute KJV editions into the United Kingdom because of the jurisdiction-specific rights associated with the Authorized Version.
