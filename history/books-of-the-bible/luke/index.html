Luke

Luke is one of the four canonical Gospels—ancient biographies that proclaim the good news about Jesus’ life, death, and resurrection. Each Gospel shapes eyewitness memories and early tradition into a distinctive theological portrait through narrative structure, Old Testament echoes, and teaching blocks. Luke situates Jesus within Israel’s story and God’s kingdom, drawing readers to discipleship. Scholars consider audience, sources, and literary features (parables, miracle stories, passion narrative) to hear the evangelist’s voice. For the church, Luke is the doorway to Christian faith and practice: it orders the calendar, governs preaching, and steadies hope in the crucified and risen Lord.
